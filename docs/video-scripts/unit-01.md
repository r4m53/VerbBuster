# Lesson 01 — Night Ninja's Challenge

## Production guide

- Theme: PJ Masks
- Rival: Night Ninja
- BASE forms: blue
- SIMPLE PAST forms: coral
- PAST PARTICIPLE forms: lime
- Keep the highlighted verb on screen while its sentence is narrated.

## Scene 1 — Rival entrance

**Night Ninja:** “The night is my playground, Verb Buster. Show me you can master these eight moves before the moon disappears!”

## Scene 2 — Today’s roster

## Scenes 3–10 — Verb training
