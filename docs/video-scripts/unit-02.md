# Lesson 02 — Luna Girl's Challenge

## Production guide

- Theme: PJ Masks
- Rival: Luna Girl
- BASE forms: blue
- SIMPLE PAST forms: coral
- PAST PARTICIPLE forms: lime
- Keep the highlighted verb on screen while its sentence is narrated.

## Scene 1 — Rival entrance

**Luna Girl:** “Moon power makes everything tricky. Can your verb skills shine brighter than my lunar tricks?”

## Scenes 3–10 — Verb training

- **PARTICIPLE · lime:** They have said the answer.
