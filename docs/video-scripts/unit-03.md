# Lesson 03 — Romeo's Boss Challenge

## Production guide

- Theme: PJ Masks
- Rival: Romeo
- BASE forms: blue
- SIMPLE PAST forms: coral
- PAST PARTICIPLE forms: lime
- Keep the highlighted verb on screen while its sentence is narrated.

## Scene 1 — Rival entrance

**Romeo:** “My newest invention scrambles verbs! Let us see whether your brain can put every form back in order.”

## Scenes 3–10 — Verb training

### 1. EAT — ATE — EATEN

- **BASE · blue:** We eat lunch at noon.
- **PAST · coral:** I ate an apple yesterday.
- **PARTICIPLE · lime:** She has eaten her sandwich.
- Narration: “eat, ate, eaten.”
