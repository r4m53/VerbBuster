# Lesson 04 — Mayor Humdinger's Challenge

## Production guide

- Theme: Paw Patrol
- Rival: Mayor Humdinger
- BASE forms: blue
- SIMPLE PAST forms: coral
- PAST PARTICIPLE forms: lime
- Keep the highlighted verb on screen while its sentence is narrated.

## Scene 1 — Rival entrance

**Mayor Humdinger:** “No pups can save you from my challenge. Earn every verb before I claim the victory!”

## Scenes 3–10 — Verb training

### 1. READ — READ — READ

- **BASE · blue:** I read a chapter every night.
- **PAST · coral:** We read a funny story yesterday.
- **PARTICIPLE · lime:** She has read that book twice.
- Narration: “read, read, read.”
