# Lesson 05 — Sweetie's Challenge

## Production guide

- Theme: Paw Patrol
- Rival: Sweetie
- BASE forms: blue
- SIMPLE PAST forms: coral
- PAST PARTICIPLE forms: lime
- Keep the highlighted verb on screen while its sentence is narrated.

## Scene 1 — Rival entrance

**Sweetie:** “A royal challenge deserves a royal champion. Show me whether you are worthy of these eight verbs!”

## Scene 2 — Today’s roster

run — ran — run

ride — rode — ridden

drive — drove — driven

fly — flew — flown

swim — swam — swum

leave — left — left

become — became — become

## Scenes 3–10 — Verb training

### 1. RUN — RAN — RUN

- **BASE · blue:** I run around the track.
- **PAST · coral:** We ran to the finish line.
- **PARTICIPLE · lime:** He has run five laps.
- Narration: “run, ran, run.”

### 2. RIDE — RODE — RIDDEN

- **BASE · blue:** I ride my bike after school.
- **PAST · coral:** Ella rode a horse yesterday.
- **PARTICIPLE · lime:** We have ridden that trail.
- Narration: “ride, rode, ridden.”

### 3. DRIVE — DROVE — DRIVEN

- **BASE · blue:** My parents drive to work.
- **PAST · coral:** Dad drove us to the park.
- **PARTICIPLE · lime:** She has driven this road before.
- Narration: “drive, drove, driven.”

### 4. FLY — FLEW — FLOWN

- **BASE · blue:** Birds fly over the trees.
- **PAST · coral:** The kite flew very high.
- **PARTICIPLE · lime:** We have flown in an airplane.
- Narration: “fly, flew, flown.”

### 5. SWIM — SWAM — SWUM

- **BASE · blue:** I swim every Saturday.
- **PAST · coral:** They swam across the pool.
- **PARTICIPLE · lime:** She has swum in the ocean.
- Narration: “swim, swam, swum.”

### 6. FALL — FELL — FALLEN

- **BASE · blue:** Leaves fall from the trees.
- **PAST · coral:** The ball fell off the table.

- Narration: “fall, fell, fallen.”

### 7. LEAVE — LEFT — LEFT

- **BASE · blue:** We leave home at eight.
- **PAST · coral:** The bus left early.

### 8. BECOME — BECAME — BECOME

- **BASE · blue:** Caterpillars become butterflies.

- Narration: “become, became, become.”

## Scene 11 — Rival’s final challenge

**Sweetie:** “You handled my royal test. Now win the Battle like a true champion!”
