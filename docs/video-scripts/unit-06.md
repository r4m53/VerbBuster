# Lesson 06 — Copycat's Boss Challenge

## Production guide

- Theme: Paw Patrol
- Rival: Copycat
- BASE forms: blue
- SIMPLE PAST forms: coral
- PAST PARTICIPLE forms: lime
- Keep the highlighted verb on screen while its sentence is narrated.

## Scene 1 — Rival entrance

**Copycat:** “I can copy every move you make. Can you remember the forms when I mix them all together?”

## Scenes 3–10 — Verb training

### 1. BEGIN — BEGAN — BEGUN

- **BASE · blue:** I begin the game after lunch.
- **PAST · coral:** Yesterday, I began the game after lunch.
- **PARTICIPLE · lime:** I have begun the game after lunch.
- Narration: “begin, began, begun.”
