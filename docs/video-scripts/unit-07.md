# Lesson 07 — Admiral Zhao's Challenge

## Production guide

- Theme: Avatar
- Rival: Admiral Zhao
- BASE forms: blue
- SIMPLE PAST forms: coral
- PAST PARTICIPLE forms: lime
- Keep the highlighted verb on screen while its sentence is narrated.

## Scene 1 — Rival entrance

**Admiral Zhao:** “Discipline wins every fight. Control these eight verbs, or my challenge will stop your advance!”

## Scene 2 — Today’s roster

## Scenes 3–10 — Verb training

### 1. SING — SANG — SUNG

- **BASE · blue:** I sing our favorite song together.
- **PAST · coral:** Yesterday, I sang our favorite song together.
- **PARTICIPLE · lime:** I have sung our favorite song together.
- Narration: “sing, sang, sung.”

### 2. RING — RANG — RUNG
