# Lesson 08 — Azula's Challenge

## Production guide

- Theme: Avatar
- Rival: Azula
- BASE forms: blue
- SIMPLE PAST forms: coral
- PAST PARTICIPLE forms: lime
- Keep the highlighted verb on screen while its sentence is narrated.

## Scene 1 — Rival entrance

**Azula:** “One mistake is all I need. Keep your focus and master every form, Verb Buster.”

## Scenes 3–10 — Verb training

### 1. BLOW — BLEW — BLOWN

- **BASE · blue:** I blow bubbles in the backyard.
- **PAST · coral:** Yesterday, I blew bubbles in the backyard.
- **PARTICIPLE · lime:** I have blown bubbles in the backyard.
- Narration: “blow, blew, blown.”
