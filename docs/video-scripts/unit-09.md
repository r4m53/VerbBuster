# Lesson 09 — Fire Lord Ozai's Boss Challenge

## Production guide

- Theme: Avatar
- Rival: Fire Lord Ozai
- BASE forms: blue
- SIMPLE PAST forms: coral
- PAST PARTICIPLE forms: lime
- Keep the highlighted verb on screen while its sentence is narrated.

## Scene 1 — Rival entrance

**Fire Lord Ozai:** “Power is earned through mastery. Prove that your command of verbs is strong enough to continue.”

## Scenes 3–10 — Verb training

### 1. BEND — BENT — BENT

- **BASE · blue:** I bend the wire into a circle.
- **PAST · coral:** Yesterday, I bent the wire into a circle.
- **PARTICIPLE · lime:** I have bent the wire into a circle.
- Narration: “bend, bent, bent.”
