# Lesson 10 — Draco Malfoy's Challenge

## Production guide

- Theme: Harry Potter
- Rival: Draco Malfoy
- BASE forms: blue
- SIMPLE PAST forms: coral
- PAST PARTICIPLE forms: lime
- Keep the highlighted verb on screen while its sentence is narrated.

## Scene 1 — Rival entrance

**Draco Malfoy:** “Think you know these spells—I mean verbs? I doubt you can master all eight without making a mistake.”

## Scenes 3–10 — Verb training

### 1. LEAD — LED — LED

- **BASE · blue:** I lead the team through each challenge.
- **PAST · coral:** Yesterday, I led the team through each challenge.
- **PARTICIPLE · lime:** I have led the team through each challenge.
- Narration: “lead, led, led.”

### 2. LEND — LENT — LENT
