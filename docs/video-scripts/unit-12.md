# Lesson 12 — Voldemort's Boss Challenge

## Production guide

- Theme: Harry Potter
- Rival: Voldemort
- BASE forms: blue
- SIMPLE PAST forms: coral
- PAST PARTICIPLE forms: lime
- Keep the highlighted verb on screen while its sentence is narrated.

## Scene 1 — Rival entrance

**Voldemort:** “Every word carries power. Master these forms, or your journey ends here.”

## Scenes 3–10 — Verb training
