# Lesson 13 — Cassios's Challenge

## Production guide

- Theme: Zodiac Knights
- Rival: Cassios
- BASE forms: blue
- SIMPLE PAST forms: coral
- PAST PARTICIPLE forms: lime
- Keep the highlighted verb on screen while its sentence is narrated.

## Scene 1 — Rival entrance

**Cassios:** “Strength alone will crush your little word game. Show me these verbs can stand against me!”

## Scenes 3–10 — Verb training

### 1. STICK — STUCK — STUCK

- **BASE · blue:** I stick notes onto the classroom board.
- **PAST · coral:** Yesterday, I stuck notes onto the classroom board.
- **PARTICIPLE · lime:** I have stuck notes onto the classroom board.
- Narration: “stick, stuck, stuck.”

### 2. STING — STUNG — STUNG
