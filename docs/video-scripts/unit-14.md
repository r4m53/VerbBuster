# Lesson 14 — Cancer Deathmask's Challenge

## Production guide

- Theme: Zodiac Knights
- Rival: Cancer Deathmask
- BASE forms: blue
- SIMPLE PAST forms: coral
- PAST PARTICIPLE forms: lime
- Keep the highlighted verb on screen while its sentence is narrated.

## Scene 1 — Rival entrance

**Cancer Deathmask:** “Fear can break concentration. Keep your courage while these eight verbs change before you.”

## Scenes 3–10 — Verb training

### 1. BIND — BOUND — BOUND

- **BASE · blue:** I bind the pages into a small book.
- **PAST · coral:** Yesterday, I bound the pages into a small book.
- **PARTICIPLE · lime:** I have bound the pages into a small book.
- Narration: “bind, bound, bound.”
