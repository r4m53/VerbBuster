# Lesson 15 — Hades's Boss Challenge

## Production guide

- Theme: Zodiac Knights
- Rival: Hades
- BASE forms: blue
- SIMPLE PAST forms: coral
- PAST PARTICIPLE forms: lime
- Keep the highlighted verb on screen while its sentence is narrated.

## Scene 1 — Rival entrance

**Hades:** “Even heroes tremble before the final darkness. Master every form if you wish to pass.”
