# Lesson 16 — Crocodine's Challenge

## Production guide

- Theme: Dragon Quest: Dai
- Rival: Crocodine
- BASE forms: blue
- SIMPLE PAST forms: coral
- PAST PARTICIPLE forms: lime
- Keep the highlighted verb on screen while its sentence is narrated.

## Scene 1 — Rival entrance

**Crocodine:** “My strength can split mountains. Can your memory hold together through all eight verbs?”

## Scenes 3–10 — Verb training
