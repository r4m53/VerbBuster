## Production guide

- Theme: Dragon Quest: Dai

## Scenes 3–10 — Verb training

### 1. ARISE — AROSE — ARISEN

- **BASE · blue:** I arise to face unexpected challenges.
- **PAST · coral:** Yesterday, I arose to face unexpected challenges.
- **PARTICIPLE · lime:** I have arisen to face unexpected challenges.
- Narration: “arise, arose, arisen.”
