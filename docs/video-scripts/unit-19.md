# Lesson 19 — Raditz's Challenge

## Production guide

- Theme: Dragon Ball
- Rival: Raditz
- BASE forms: blue
- SIMPLE PAST forms: coral
- PAST PARTICIPLE forms: lime
- Keep the highlighted verb on screen while its sentence is narrated.

## Scene 1 — Rival entrance

**Raditz:** “Welcome to the battlefield. If these eight verbs slow you down, you will never reach the next level!”

## Scenes 3–10 — Verb training

### 1. OVERTAKE — OVERTOOK — OVERTAKEN

- **BASE · blue:** I overtake slower runners near the finish.
- **PAST · coral:** Yesterday, I overtook slower runners near the finish.
- **PARTICIPLE · lime:** I have overtaken slower runners near the finish.
- Narration: “overtake, overtook, overtaken.”

### 2. REDO — REDID — REDONE

- **BASE · blue:** We redo work that needs careful correction.
- **PAST · coral:** Last week, we redid work that needs careful correction.

- Narration: “redo, redid, redone.”

### 3. REPAY — REPAID — REPAID
